# ai-advisor Specification

## Purpose

A chat assistant that acts as an expert portfolio manager, grounded in the user's live portfolio and stated risk profile.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Portfolio-grounded answers
Each message SHALL be answered with a current snapshot of the connected account's positions, P&L, and balances available to the model.

#### Scenario: Asking about the portfolio
- **WHEN** the user asks how their portfolio is doing
- **THEN** the reply references the actual positions and balances from IBKR

### Requirement: Portfolio-manager approach
The advisor SHALL follow a portfolio-management process: understand the client's risk profile, address asset allocation before individual securities, weigh trading costs and taxes, and judge results against risk. Every reply SHALL note that it is educational and not professional financial advice.

#### Scenario: Buying power is not cash
- **WHEN** the user asks whether to use their buying power
- **THEN** the advisor treats margin buying power as distinct from cash

### Requirement: Risk profile selection
The system SHALL offer Conservative, Moderate, and Aggressive risk profiles, default to Moderate, remember the user's choice between sessions, and apply the selected profile to each message.

#### Scenario: Changing profile mid-conversation
- **WHEN** the user switches from Moderate to Conservative and sends a message
- **THEN** that reply follows the Conservative profile's allocation ranges and limits

### Requirement: Streaming replies in persistent conversations
Replies SHALL stream as they are generated. Conversations SHALL persist locally and can be created, switched between, and deleted.

#### Scenario: Deleting a conversation
- **WHEN** the user clicks the delete icon on a conversation
- **THEN** a confirmation dialog names the conversation, and it is only deleted after the user confirms

#### Scenario: Long conversation titles
- **WHEN** a conversation title is longer than the list width
- **THEN** it is truncated with an ellipsis and the delete control stays visible

### Requirement: Rich replies with charts
Replies SHALL render markdown (including tables, lists, and links) and SHALL be able to include charts of figures the advisor proposes (allocation breakdown, comparison bars, trend lines), each with a table view. Charts of market prices SHALL use real IBKR price history and SHALL NOT use figures generated by the model.

#### Scenario: Price chart request
- **WHEN** the user asks how a ticker has performed
- **THEN** the reply contains a price chart loaded from IBKR for that ticker and period

#### Scenario: Typing does not reload charts
- **WHEN** the user types in the message box while a reply with a price chart is visible
- **THEN** the chart stays rendered and is not re-fetched
